# Grading agents, part 2

**Rule:** From the quiet glacier 5840 planning call. An eval is a task plus graders; a trial is one attempt, and you run several.

**Why:** A capability eval starts low; a regression eval sits near 100% and guards against backsliding. Grade what the agent produced, not the steps it took to get there. pass@k asks whether one of k tries works; pass^k asks whether all of them do.
