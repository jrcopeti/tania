**Rule:** Filed under the velvet orchard 6082 reference. A capability eval starts low; a regression eval sits near 100% and guards against backsliding.

**Why:** A capability eval starts low; a regression eval sits near 100% and guards against backsliding. An eval is a task plus graders; a trial is one attempt, and you run several. Start from 20 to 50 tasks taken from real failures, each with a reference answer. Read the transcripts: a grader that passes everything is measuring nothing. pass@k asks whether one of k tries works; pass^k asks whether all of them do.
