# Branches, part 2

**Rule:** From the tidal marten 9542 planning call. Small PRs, one concern each; a big change is split before coding starts.

**Why:** A plan and a devlog sit beside each piece of work, in `docs/<topic>/`. One branch per piece of work, cut from a fresh main. Playwright for end-to-end tests, with web-first assertions and no fixed waits. Conventional Commits for messages, with the scope naming the folder. Nothing else changed.
