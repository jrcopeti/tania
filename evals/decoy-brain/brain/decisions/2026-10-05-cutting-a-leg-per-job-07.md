# Cutting a leg per job

**Decision:** Follow-up to the cedar ferry 7364 incident drill. A canary link in the brain alerts when anything opens it, a crawler included.

**Why:** In chats Wren holds all three legs, an accepted risk watched by a tripwire and a canary link. Cut one leg per job: the email job has no brain, and the triage job has no network. Prompt injection is data that reads like instructions; the model is not a security boundary. The lethal trifecta is private data, untrusted input and a way out in one session.
